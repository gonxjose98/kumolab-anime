- Question: why does a page with one line of text on an anime clip get millions of views while KumoLab's look-alike reels get 100 to 200?

## TL;DR

1. They post things people feel, not things people should know. Two lanes only: tearjerker romance (blind girl, dying girl, first kiss) and slapstick comedy (Gintama). Zero news, zero trailers.
2. Every reel is watchable by an English speaker with the sound off: English-subbed footage, or an English pop song over a wordless montage. KumoLab: 0 of 12 sampled October frames had English subtitles.
3. The text is a viewer's reaction, not a headline. Lowercase, "bro", one emoji, says what to feel. No logo, no show title, no studio credit on screen.
4. The reels go far beyond followers: median 157K plays on 19.6K followers (8x). KumoLab median 262 plays on 2,437 followers (0.1x). That points to KumoLab not getting recommended at all, not just weaker hooks.
5. Like rate is 7.8% (median) vs KumoLab's 1.15%. People who see their reels react to them, which is the signal that gets them pushed further.

Why it hit (my read, ranked):

1. Instant premise, frame one. A girl with a white cane is readable in under a second, even with sound off. The text then tells you how to feel about it. No setup, no context needed.
2. One big universal emotion: tender plus sad. "She couldn't see the world, but she always saw the best in it" is a quote-able, share-able line. It works for people who have never watched anime, which is how a 19.6K page gets 4M plays.
3. A song that carries feeling without language. Soft English indie over a wordless montage means no subtitles have to be read to follow it.
4. Loops cleanly. Static text, gentle montage, no title card or end card, so the replay is seamless.
5. Comment bait is implicit, not asked for: the premise makes people tag someone, say "what anime is this", or argue about the ending. 1,445 comments with no CTA anywhere.
6. Fresh show with no existing clip saturation. It is a 2026 title, so the scenes were new to most viewers.

They then milked the same show: 9 reels of Love Unseen in the sample (median 172K, top 3.99M, 702K, 518K, 363K), including a "Live action: / Anime:" split comparison (702K).

## Gap vs KumoLab: evidence vs guess

Evidence (observed directly in the data):
- Reach beyond followers: zyxx median plays 8x followers, KumoLab 0.1x. KumoLab's best of 48 reels is 13,467 (a Mushoku Tensei S3 trailer).
- Content type: KumoLab's last 30 reels are about 20 news/trailer reels (many raw official trailers, 180 to 190s long, often posted 3 in 10 minutes, for example 2026-09-26 23:58, 09-27 00:03, 00:08) and about 8 "Kumo Picks" scene reels in October. zyxx posts zero news.
- Language: 0 of 12 sampled KumoLab October frames show English subtitles (some show Japanese only). zyxx: nearly every frame has English subs, or the reel runs on an English song.
- Audio: KumoLab 28 of 29 original audio (Japanese dialogue or trailer score). zyxx romance lane runs English romance-edit songs.
- Look: KumoLab's Rising Clouds widescreen frame has a logo, a gold "KUMO PICKS" label, a serif headline with a gold italic word, cloud banks, and show title + studio + year at the bottom. zyxx has plain text on black and nothing else.
- Hook voice: KumoLab headlines read as editorial ("The moment Yuta entered the classroom", "Sheena's cute scenes", "Blue Box Season 2 is now streaming"). zyxx reads as a viewer reacting ("bro really...", "It hurts knowing...").
- Engagement: KumoLab like rate 1.15%, 0 to 3 comments. zyxx 7.8%, dozens to hundreds of comments.
- Captions: both use 5 hashtags. KumoLab writes hook + CTA ("Send this to your JJK friend"); zyxx writes a keyword-rich synopsis with show and character names and no CTA.
- Consistency: zyxx repeats a few shows many times (Gintama 20x). KumoLab changes show almost every post.

Guesses (likely, not proven):
- Account-level demotion. 0.1x followers is below what even a weak reel gets when it is shown to followers and recommended. A history of reposted official trailers in bursts is exactly what IG's originality rules demote. Check: IG app > Settings > Account Status > "Recommendation eligibility" / "Content that can't be recommended". This is the first thing to check because no format change wins if reels are not eligible for recommendation.
- The branded frame reads as a publisher or an ad, so viewers swipe in the first second. The plain zyxx layout reads like a friend's share.
- Japanese audio without subtitles kills completion for the US/English audience IG is testing the reel on.
- The serif headline is harder to read at phone size in the first second than plain sans.
- Mixing news, trailers and picks confuses IG about who the audience is; two fixed lanes teach it fast.

## Risks (one line each)

- zyxx reposts copyrighted episode footage with fansub-style subtitles. Copyright claims are a real risk for KumoLab if it copies that sourcing; official English-subbed clips (Crunchyroll/Netflix/Muse YouTube) are the safer source.
